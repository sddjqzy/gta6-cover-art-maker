Vice Cover is an offline browser app that turns your photos into GTA-style multi-panel cover art, ready to post. Pick two photos (the built-in pier and boulevard samples, or upload your own), write a caption and a tagline, choose a panel layout — trio (one big panel plus two small), quad (2 × 2) or duo (side by side) — pick a colour grade (sunset, neon or teal) and a post size (square 1080 × 1080, portrait 1024 × 1280 or wide 1280 × 720), watch the live preview update as you change anything, and export the finished cover as a PNG to share. Everything runs locally in the browser; your photos never leave your machine. The bold caption lettering uses Anton, an open-licensed font shipped with the app.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser and start with the two sample photos — no upload needed to try it.
